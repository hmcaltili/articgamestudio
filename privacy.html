Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 14, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Hamza Mücahit Çaltılı (trading as Artic Game Studio, hereinafter referred to as "we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, processes, and protects your information when you play our mini-games and applications on the TikTok Mini Games platform (the "Service").

We are committed to respecting and protecting the privacy of all players in compliance with global data protection regulations, including the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA).

1Information We Collect

Our mini-games are designed to minimize data collection. We do not collect or request sensitive personal identification details (such as real names, physical addresses, phone numbers, or government IDs).

  • Non-Personal Technical Data: Standard gameplay analytics, session timestamps, device hardware characteristics, operating system versions, and language settings automatically processed to ensure game performance.
  • Game Progress & Save Data: High scores, levels unlocked, and in-game achievements stored locally on your device or linked anonymously via TikTok User ID.
  • TikTok Platform Data: Pseudonymized identifier (TikTok OpenID) provided by the TikTok Mini Games platform to maintain session state and leaderboard functionalities.

2Third-Party Advertising & Monetization

We utilize authorized third-party ad networks (including TikTok Ads SDK / ByteDance Monetization Platform) to serve advertisements within the game (such as Rewarded Video Ads and Interstitial Ads).

These third-party SDKs may process anonymized device identifiers (e.g., GAID/IDFA) and interaction metrics solely for ad delivery, fraud prevention, and performance attribution. We do not sell or share your personal data with any third parties for independent marketing purposes.

3Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

Our services are intended for a general audience. We do not knowingly collect, store, or solicit personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in the European Union). If we become aware that personal information of a child has been collected inadvertently, we take prompt measures to delete such data from our records.

4Data Security

We apply industry-standard administrative and technical security safeguards (including HTTPS/SSL encryption protocols) to protect any information processed through our games against unauthorized access, loss, alteration, or misuse.

5Your Data Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you have the right to request access to, rectification of, or deletion of any data associated with your game session. To exercise your privacy rights, please contact us using the details below.
